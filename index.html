Logo design, drawn with nothing but boxes

Two brand marks rebuilt from scratch. Every edge is an absolutely positioned <span>, skewed and layered into shape.

Aflaria

Ten spans build the mark: two skewed bars form the “A”, black copies punch out the counters, an arrow rises through the middle. Sized entirely in rem, so one root font-size scales it to any screen without moving a coordinate.

  • skewX()
  • z-index layering
  • inset shadows

Netflix

Three red bars grow out of the baseline on a staggered delay. The middle one is skewed 22deg from its top-left corner and dropped behind a shadow to fake the fold.

  • @keyframes
  • staggered delays
  • transform-origin
